Toy Store Return, Exchange, and Refund Policy

1. General Return Policy
We want you and your family to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Unopened and unused items in their original, factory-sealed packaging can be returned within 60 days of the original purchase date for a full refund or store credit. Receipts, gift receipts, or digital proof of purchase are strictly required for all returns. If a gift receipt is provided, the return will be processed as store credit only.

2. Damaged or Defective Items
We take quality control seriously, but occasionally items are damaged during the shipping process. Returns for items damaged in transit or defective out of the box must be initiated within 30 days of receipt. A full refund will be issued to the original payment method, or a replacement will be shipped at no extra cost. Please provide photographic evidence of the damage to our customer service portal when initiating the return. For safety reasons, do not return broken glass or shattered hard plastic directly without speaking to a support agent first, as they will provide specialized disposal and replacement instructions.

3. Holiday Return Extension
To make gift-giving easier, items purchased between November 1st and December 24th are eligible for an extended return period. These items may be returned until January 31st of the following year, provided they meet the general condition requirements stated in Section 1.

4. Exceptions and Non-Returnable Items
The following items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable:
- Clearance Items: All items marked as "Clearance," "Last Chance," or "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive fundamentally damaged.
- Collectibles: Opened trading cards, blind boxes, and limited-edition serialized figurines.
- Digital Goods: Gift cards, downloadable software, and digital video game codes.

5. Refund Processing Times
Once your return is received and inspected at our fulfillment center (usually within 3-5 business days of delivery), your refund will be processed. Please allow up to 7-10 additional business days for the credit to appear on your credit card or bank statement, depending on your financial institution's policies.
