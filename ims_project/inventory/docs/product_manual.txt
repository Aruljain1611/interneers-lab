Comprehensive Product Warranty and Care Manual

1. General Maintenance and Care
Proper maintenance extends the life of toys and ensures child safety. 
- Wooden Toys: Wipe clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not submerge in water, as this may warp the wood and strip non-toxic paints.
- Plush and Fabric Toys: Machine wash only if the tag explicitly indicates "Machine Washable." Otherwise, spot clean with a pet-safe, child-safe upholstery cleaner.
- Electronic Toys: Keep electronic toys away from water, extreme heat, and direct prolonged sunlight. Always remove batteries if the toy will not be used for an extended period (more than 30 days) to prevent battery acid leakage, which voids all warranties.

2. Manufacturer Specific Warranties
Different brands carry varying warranty terms. Our store acts as a facilitator for these manufacturer warranties for the first 30 days; afterward, claims must be made directly with the brand.

2a. Lego Products: Lego products, including the popular Lego Castle set (SKU: LG-CSTL-01), are covered by a standard 1-year (12-month) manufacturer's warranty against defects. This warranty covers missing pieces right out of the box or defective plastic molds. It does NOT cover pieces lost by the user after assembly, nor does it cover damage from pets. To claim missing pieces, visit the manufacturer's website directly and enter the set number.

2b. Action Figures: Standard action figures from licensed brands (Marvel, Star Wars, DC) come with a 90-day limited warranty covering joint breakages under normal play conditions. Excessive force or deliberate modification voids this warranty.

2c. Smart Toys and Drones: Wi-Fi or Bluetooth-enabled toys carry a strict 6-month warranty covering internal hardware failure. Firmware updates must be applied before submitting a warranty claim.

3. Assembly Best Practices
When assembling large playsets, always inventory the parts against the instruction manual before beginning. Do not over-tighten screws on plastic components, as this will cause stress fractures over time.
